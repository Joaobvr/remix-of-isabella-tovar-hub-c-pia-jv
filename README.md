# Remix of Isabella Tovar Hub - Cópia JV

Create a premium, mobile-first "Link in Bio" hub landing page for Dr. Isabella Tovar, a medical doctor specialized in women's health, longevity, vitality, and aesthetic medicine. The design must be elegant, sophisticated, and minimalist, using a color palette of off-white, soft gold/champagne, and deep elegant green or navy (to convey medical authority and premium aesthetics). 



The layout should be optimized for mobile devices but look great on desktop as well. It will serve as a portal directing users to specific treatment landing pages.



### Structure and Content Requirements:



1. **Header Section:**

   - A circular profile picture placeholder at the top center (with a subtle gold border).

   - **Name:** "Dra. Isabella Tovar" in an elegant serif font (like Playfair Display or Lora).

   - **Subtitle/Bio:** "Medicina focada na saúde, longevidade, vitalidade e beleza da mulher." in a clean sans-serif font (like Montserrat or Lato).

   - **Short Intro Text:** "Para proporcionar um cuidado feminino completo e integrativo, atuo em diferentes áreas essenciais para a sua melhor versão. Escolha abaixo a área do seu interesse:" (Make this text readable, centered, and with a soft gray/charcoal color).



2. **Navigation/Portal Links (The Core Features):**

   - Instead of standard small buttons, create large, elegant, full-width rectangular "cards" or "banners" (faixas) stacked vertically.

   - Each banner should have a subtle hover effect (slight lift or glow) and an arrow icon (→) on the right side to indicate it's a clickable link.

   - Each banner must include an elegant, minimalist icon on the left representing the service.

   - **Banner 1:** "Emagrecimento Saudável e Metabólico"

   - **Banner 2:** "FF Sculpt (Remodelação Corporal)"

   - **Banner 3:** "Regeneração e Estética Íntima"

   - **Banner 4:** "Terapias com Suplementos e Injetáveis"

   - **Banner 5:** "Terapias e Implantes Hormonais"

   - **Banner 6:** "Acompanhamento Ginecológico"



3. **Footer Section:**

   - Social media icons (Instagram, WhatsApp) linking to her profiles.

   - Address/Location info (optional placeholder: "Atendimento Exclusivo em [Cidade]").

   - A subtle copyright text at the very bottom: "© Dra. Isabella Tovar - CRM [Número]"



### Design & Technical Specifications:

- **Framework/Styling:** Use Tailwind CSS.

- **Vibe:** "Quiet Luxury", High-ticket clinic, Premium medical care. 

- **Animations:** Add a smooth fade-in animation when the page loads, and a gentle scale transition when hovering over the service banners.

- **Responsiveness:** Must be perfectly responsive. On mobile, the banners should take up 90% of the screen width with rounded corners (e.g., `rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`) and soft shadows (`shadow-md`).

- **Icons:** Use Lucide React icons (e.g., Activity, Sparkles, Heart, Syringe, etc.) to match each service elegantly.
